Who We Are

We are an Accounting Practising and Consulting Firm. Our partners and staff have been exposed to vast training, technical knowledge and many years of cognate experience in the field.

We comply with standards, with a record of zero litigations since inception. As a member of the Public Practitioners Forum, District Society of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria, and the Network of Peer Review Group of some accounting practising firms, we have built a culture based on professionalism.

Registration and Credentials

01

Company Registration

The company was formally registered on 20 October 1993 under the provisions of the Company and Allied Matters Act, 1990. Commencement of business dates back to January 1991.

02

Auditor-General Registration

Our company is registered with the Office of the Auditor-General of the Federation, qualifying us to audit government parastatals, boards, agencies and corporations. The company is also registered with the Office of the Auditor-General of the State.

03

Licence to Practise

A licence to practise as Chartered Accountants was obtained from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN), upon meeting the conditions for issuance.

04

FIRS Registration

For VAT administration, our company is registered with the Federal Inland Revenue Service.
