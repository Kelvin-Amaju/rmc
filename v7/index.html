01What services does RMC provide?

Our work includes audit and assurance, tax consultancy, accounting systems, accounting records, inventory management, management information systems, recovery of excess bank charges and wider consultancy.

02When was the firm established?

Rheriene Mabamidje & Co. commenced business in January 1991 and was formally registered on 20 October 1993.

03Is the firm licensed to practise?

Yes. The firm has obtained a licence to practise from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN).

04Does RMC provide accounting system support?

Yes. We design and implement accounting systems, update accounting records and help computerise appropriate accounting processes.

05How do I start an engagement?

Contact the firm to discuss your needs. We can review the scope of work and advise on the next steps.
