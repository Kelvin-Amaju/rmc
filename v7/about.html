RMC / OUR STORY / 1991–TODAY

Accounting is about much more than the numbers. It is about the confidence people place in them.

Rheriene Mabamidje & Co. commenced business in January 1991 and was formally registered on 20 October 1993. Since then, the firm has served organisations with professional accounting, audit, tax and consultancy services.

Our partners and staff combine technical knowledge with practical experience to support informed decisions and responsible financial management.
