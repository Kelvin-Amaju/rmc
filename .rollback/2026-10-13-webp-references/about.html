Registration with Corporate Affairs Commission

The company was formally registered under the provisions of the Company and Allied Matters Act, 1990 on the 20th day of October, 1993 although its commencement of business dated back to January, 1991.

Registration with the Office the of Auditor General of the Federation

Our company is duly registered with the Office of the Auditor-General of the Federation. This qualifies us to carry out Audit of Government Parastatals, Board, Agencies and Corporations. Our company is also registered with the Office of the Auditor-General of the State.
