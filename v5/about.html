Who we are

Professional Registration and Experience

The company was formally registered on 20 October 1993, with commencement of business dating back to January 1991.

The firm is registered with the Office of the Auditor-General of the Federation and the Office of the Auditor-General of the State.

A licence to practise as Chartered Accountants was obtained from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN).

For VAT administration, the firm is registered with the Federal Inland Revenue Service.
